## Project Overview

Dear Annex Holdings,

We are pleased to submit this bid proposal for the replacement of the 4,000 sq ft flat roof on your office annex. This project involves removing the existing EPDM membrane and installing a new insulation and copper roofing system, including tear-off, disposal, insulation installation, standing-seam copper panels, edge flashing, and roof drain replacement.

## Scope of Work & Materials

The scope of work includes:

- EPDM membrane tear-off and disposal
- Installation of rigid polyiso insulation board (3 inches thick)
- Cast-iron roof drain replacement
- Standing-seam copper roofing system installation
- Copper edge flashing to match roofing panels

## Assumptions & Exclusions

This bid is based on the following assumptions:

- Existing roof structure is sound and suitable for the new roofing system
- Site access is available and unobstructed during construction
- Work will be performed during normal business hours

Exclusions:

- Building permit fees
- Subsurface rock excavation or unexpected structural repairs
- Hazardous material handling (e.g., asbestos abatement)
