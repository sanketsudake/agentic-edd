## Project Overview

Northside Dental Partners,

We are pleased to present this bid proposal for the interior build-out of your dental clinic. This project involves transforming a 2,500 sq ft shell space into a fully functional dental facility with treatment rooms, reception area, and restroom. The scope includes metal stud framing, soundproof insulation, drywall installation, and acoustic drop ceilings throughout the space, with special attention to ADA compliance and acoustic requirements for treatment rooms.
