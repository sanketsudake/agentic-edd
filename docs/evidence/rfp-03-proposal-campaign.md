## Project Overview

Dear Supply Yard LLC,

We are pleased to submit this bid proposal for the construction of a 400 linear foot by 8 foot high masonry security wall at your supply yard facility. This proposal provides a comprehensive solution using standard concrete masonry units with vertical rebar reinforcement, grout fill for reinforced cells, and a smooth concrete cap finish to deliver a durable and secure perimeter barrier.

## Scope of Work & Materials

The project includes the following materials and installation:

| Description | Quantity | Unit | Unit Cost | Line Total |
|-------------|----------|------|------------|------------|
| 8x8x16 concrete masonry units (CMU) | 3,600 | each | $3.20 | $11,520.00 |
| #5 rebar vertical | 150 | linear_ft | $1.10 | $165.00 |
| solid grout fill for reinforced cells | 4.8 | cu_yd | $190.00 | $912.00 |
| standard smooth concrete cap | 400 | linear_ft | $14.00 | $5,600.00 |

Special requirements:
- Standard 8x8x16 concrete masonry units
- #5 rebar every 32 inches vertically
- Solid grout fill for all reinforced cells
- Standard smooth concrete cap
- No painting or stucco required
